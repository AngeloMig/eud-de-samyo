    How do I choose a perfume I've never smelled?

    What's the difference between Eau de Parfum and Eau de Toilette?

    Eau de Parfum has a higher concentration of perfume oils than Eau de Toilette, so it usually smells richer and lasts longer. How long any perfume lasts still depends on the perfume, your skin and the weather.
