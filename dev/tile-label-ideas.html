Hero tiles: 10 label ideas

Today the label sits on the bottle at the bottom of the tile, so on narrower screens it runs over the glass ("Stronger With You Intensely" crossing the bottle). Each idea below fixes that a different way. Hover the tiles (on a phone, the "always on" versions show).

0 Now the problem

Kicker + italic name over the bottom of the image: it collides with tall bottles and long names.

DiorSauvage Eau de Parfum Top noteLemon Emporio ArmaniStronger With You Intensely

1 Caption under the tile recommended

Like a gallery or NOHO's product cards: the image stays clean, the text sits on the stone background below in upright serif. Never collides, always readable, works on phones.

3 White pill in the corner

One short word on a soft white pill, top-left ("Sauvage", "Lemon"). Reads instantly on any colour; full names live on the product page.

Sauvage Lemon · top note Stronger With You

4 Vertical label on the edge

Small capitals running up the tile's right edge, like a spine label. Editorial, never touches the bottle, needs short names.

Dior · Sauvage Top note · Lemon Armani · SWY

5 Panel slides up on hover

No text until you hover; then a paper panel slides up from the bottom with kicker + name. On phones (no hover) the panel simply stays up.

7 Handwritten note

One word in the signature script in the tile's empty corner ("fresh", "lemon", "warm"). Charming and on-brand with the hero accent; names go to the product page.

8 Sticker tag

9 One shared caption

No text on tiles at all. One caption line under the grid shows whichever tile you hover or focus (outlined). Cleanest grid; NOHO-like.
