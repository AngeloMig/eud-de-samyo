Hero: ideas 1–6

Each idea next to the hero as it is now. All are built as theme-editor settings on the Perfume hero section and are off on your homepage until you pick them. Frames are live pages (scaled down); hover and click work inside them.

Now

The hero as it is on the homepage today (preloader skipped here so frames load fast).

1

A shopping button

"Shop fragrances" (dark button) and "Find your scent" (underlined link) under the heading, rising in with the intro. Settings: Button label/link, Second link label/link.

2

Bottle tiles link to their products

content

Set each bottle tile's Link to its product page (in the theme editor once products exist). Hovering then shows the label, lifts the bottle and grows the cursor into a "View" circle, shown here on the Sauvage tile after the intro.

A shorter intro

Left: today, with the preloader (charcoal bar, then the reveal, about 2.3 s, once per visit). Right: no preloader and a 40% faster reveal (about 1.2 s), so the page is usable sooner. Settings: Show preloader, Intro speed.

5

Tile labels on phones

Phones can't hover, so today the "Top note · Lemon" labels never show there. With this on, labels sit in each tile's corner on phones and touch screens. Setting: Always show tile labels on phones and touch screens.

6

Headline and trust points

sample text

A warmer headline ("Find the perfume that smells like you") and check-marked trust points under the slogan. The three points here are examples only: use them only if they are true for your store. Setting: Trust points (one per line).
