Decants: try before the full bottle

The idea: sell small decants (1, 2, 5 and 10 ml, poured from authentic full bottles) of 5–10 fragrances in bundles, and send people who fall in love to the full bottle through a partner (affiliate) link. Below: (1) how the homepage section could look, (2) how a customer flows through it, (3) ways to sell it, (4) the numbers: a live calculator with your costs, profit per bundle, and how many scent combinations each bundle allows. 2 · How it flows

From "I don't know what to buy" to a full bottle, with you earning at every step: the decant sale, then either a 10 ml from you or the partner's commission on the full bottle.

Discover

Homepage section, a TikTok/Reel, or the "Find your scent" quiz suggests 3 scents.

Pick a set

Choose a size and bundle; pick the scents (any mix of the 5–10 on offer).

Unbox

Vials in a Samyó envelope with a scent card each: notes, how to wear it, QR to the full bottle.

Wear a week

Day 7 email/SMS: "Which one did you love?" One tap to vote.

Upgrade

Their favourite as a 10 ml from you, with the set's price as credit (for example ₱150 off).

Own it

Or the full bottle via the partner link (labelled; you earn a commission).

3 · How to sell it

Credit toward the next step

Every set comes with a voucher worth part of its price toward a 10 ml. It makes the first purchase feel risk-free and brings people back to you instead of to a big retailer.

Themed sets, not just sizes

"Fresh for the heat", "Date night", "Office safe", "Under ₱600 for gifts". People buy a story more easily than "3 × 1 ml". Same vials, different names.

Scent of the month

A monthly 3 × 1 ml subscription (pick or surprise). Predictable income and you pour in batches. Start with a pre-order list before you commit.

Short videos

TikTok / Reels: "I tried 3 Dior samples for ₱590", blind smell tests, "which one would you wear to work?". Decants are perfect for this; link to the exact set.

Marketplaces for reach

List the Try set on Shopee / Lazada too (COD is expected in the Philippines); put the card with your site and voucher inside, so the second order comes to you.

Gifting season

A boxed 6 × 1 ml "discovery box" for Christmas, Valentine's, graduation. Higher price, same pouring work, nice unboxing.

Build-your-own 6

The best price per ml goes to the biggest bundle; show "you save X%" against single vials so people trade up from 3 to 6.

Trust first

Show it's poured from authentic bottles (photos of the bottles you pour from, batch number on every label), and say clearly you're not affiliated with the brands.
