Ten ways to choose a set

Ten takes on step 1 of the vial picker. They all use the real set prices (lowest = cheapest different scents, highest = dearest; six-vial sets after their 10%). All of them fix the same problems: prices wrapping onto two lines, the extra “.00”, the badge pushing cards down, and Travel ten alone on a second row. Click to try each one.

01Even row

All six in one row, the same height. “From ₱350” in large type with “up to ₱825” underneath, so nothing wraps. The badge sits on the card’s top edge and doesn’t push anything.

02Size first, then how many

Two small questions instead of six cards: pick a size (1, 2, 5, 10 ml), then 3 or 6. Fewer choices on screen at once; good on phones.

03Vial cards

Each card shows the actual vials, one per vial in the set, at their real size. You see “three small” or “one big” before reading anything.

04Perfumer’s price list

A printed menu: set name, dotted leader, price. Feels like a parfumerie price card, and long prices never wrap.

05Swipe cards with price per vial

Big cards in a row you can swipe. The headline is the price per vial (from ₱117 a vial); the set total sits below.

06Grouped by size

Four columns, one per vial size, with each size’s sets under it. Makes it obvious that the six-vial set is the same vials, twice as many, 10% off.

07Quiet chips

One line of chips: “Try trio · 3 × 1 ml · from ₱350”. Takes the least space and puts the scents straight after.

08Price per vial, front and centre

Three across, two rows (no orphan). The right side shows the cheapest price per vial; the set range is a quiet line under it.

09Vial dots and the saving

Small vial shapes show how many you get. Six-vial sets show the price before the 10% struck out, so the saving is clear without a badge.

10Trio or six, by size

Three cards (1 ml, 2 ml, and 5/10 ml), each with its options stacked: the trio on top, the six under it with “10% off”. Feels like choosing an upgrade.
