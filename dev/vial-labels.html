Vial labels

Choose how many labels you need for each perfume and size, then press Download PDF (to save or send the file) or Print. The sheet is A4 and every label is drawn at its real size in millimetres, ready for an Epson L300 on A4 vinyl sticker paper.

  • Test first: print on plain paper and hold it against a sticker sheet to check the labels line up.
  • Epson L300 settings: load one sticker sheet in the rear tray, printable side facing you (up). In the print window choose Paper size A4, Scale 100% / Actual size (not "Fit to page"), Margins Default; in the Epson driver choose Paper type Epson Matte (or Photo Paper Glossy for glossy vinyl) and Quality High.
  • Let it dry 15–30 minutes. The L300's ink is not waterproof and perfume contains alcohol, so cover the sheet with clear cold-laminate film before cutting, or spray-test one label first.
  • Cut along the dashed lines with a ruler and craft knife.
