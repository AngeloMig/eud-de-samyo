Packaging ideas · preview

The 10 ideas, drawn larger than real size (the real size is written under each). All in your White + gold style and fonts, and all printable on the Epson L300. Tell me the numbers you want and I'll add them to the print page with the download button.

01Perfume numbers

Every perfume gets a number, used on the vial, the card and the website. Customers reorder by number.

02Cap-top dots

An 8 mm sticker on top of each cap, so the vials can be told apart from above.

03Names under each slot

The card insert that holds the vials, with each perfume printed under its hole. Packing is quicker and the box looks curated.

04Bigger 1 ml label

If the 30 × 10 mm text prints too small, 35 × 12 mm is easier to read and still fits most 1 ml vials.

05Box sleeve (belly band)

A printed paper band around a plain kraft box. The cheapest way to make a simple box look premium (about ₱1–₱3 each).

06Scent diary card

Customers rate each scent as they try it. The QR code takes them to the full bottle (your affiliate link, labelled).

07Thank-you card with a code

A reason to come back. I can create the code in Shopify when you're ready (the amount is up to you).

08Logo test strips

Paper blotters to try a scent on paper before skin. 2–3 per box. Print on plain card and cut.

09"Glass inside · keep upright" sticker

On the outside of the shipping box, for the courier.

10Gift seal and message card

An optional gift add-on at checkout (₱30–₱50), costing about ₱3 to print.
