11Logo tape

Paper tape printed with "eau de sangyó" to close the box instead of plain tape. Print on sticker paper in strips.

12Wax-seal look sticker

A printed gold "S" that looks like a wax seal, to close the box or the insert envelope.

13Hang tag for 5 & 10 ml

A small card tag on a thread around the neck of the bigger vials. Feels like a gift.

14Wrapping paper pattern

An A4 pattern sheet to wrap gifts or line the box. Prints well on plain or kraft paper.

15Six-day discovery card

For the Discovery six: one scent per day, tick it off. Turns a box of vials into a little ritual.

16Packing slip

One A6 sheet per order: what's inside, the batch, and a check box for you when packing. Customers get a copy.

17Sender address label

Your "from" label for the shipping box, so every parcel looks branded.

18Referral card

"Give ₱50, get ₱50": customers pass a code to a friend. Needs a referral app or a code per customer in Shopify.

19"Tag us" card

Invites customers to post their unboxing on TikTok or Instagram. Free content for your sister's account.

20Box lid sticker

One big round sticker on the lid of a plain box: the simplest way to brand it.

21Vial size card

Shows the four vial sizes at real size on a card, like the size guide on your website.

22Care card

Simple storage tips so the decants stay as they should.

23Box bottom contents label

What's inside, total volume, batch and where it was packed, on the bottom of the box. Useful for gifting and records.

24QR table card

A folded card for a pop-up stall or bazaar table: scan to order or see the full collection.

25Loyalty stamp card

A stamp for every set; a reward after five. Rules and reward are up to you.

26Envelope for test strips

A small paper envelope with a round seal, holding the 2–3 test strips in each box.

27Set colour band on the label

A thin coloured strip along the bottom of the vial label shows which set it belongs to: gold for sixes, sage for trios, blush for duos.

28Seasonal seal

A limited seal for Christmas or Valentine's orders. Swap the colours each season.

29"How to find your scent" card

A simple guide for first-time decant buyers. No claims about the perfumes, just how to test them.

30Founding customer card

For your first 100 customers: a numbered card that makes them feel part of the launch.
