Vial label colours

Same minimal design in different colours. White and cream print best on an Epson L300 (less ink, no banding). Dark and coloured labels use much more ink.

Colour by size (easy packing)

One soft colour per vial size, so the right vial goes into the right set at a glance: amber for 1 ml, grey-blue for 2 ml, sage for 5 ml, blush for 10 ml.

Cap seal stickers

Stuck across the cap and the vial, so the customer can see it hasn't been opened. Round seal 18 mm (on top of a cap or the box), or a thin strip 44 × 8 mm wrapped over the cap.

Insert card

Business-card size, 90 × 55 mm (10 per A4 sheet). Front: the set and its scents. Back: how to use, safety, and that the perfumes are decanted by you (not by the brands).
