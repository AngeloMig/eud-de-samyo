Packaging ideas 41–50 · preview

10 more ideas in White + gold, drawn larger than real size (real size under each). Several of these add new ways to sell (gift cards, events, partners). Pick the numbers you like.

41Mystery scent label

For a "blind" set: the perfume name is hidden under a gold scratch-off patch. Fun to film, fun to gift.

42Blind test card

Goes with a mystery set: guess A, B and C, then open the flaps to see the answers.

43Cap ring by type

A thin ring sticker on the cap shows the type at a glance: gold for Eau de Parfum, silver for Eau de Toilette, cream for Cologne.

44Gift voucher card

A printed gift card for a Shopify gift card code (₱500, ₱1,000…). Easy present for people who don't know the person's taste.

45Scent of the month card

For a future subscription: one new decant each month, ticked off on the card.

46"Size up" card

Loved a 1 ml? The next step is a bigger vial. The QR goes straight to that perfume.

47"Tell us what you think" card

Asks for a review on your website. Keep it honest: no reward for good reviews only (if you give a thank-you, give it for any review and say so).

48Wedding & event favours

A new way to sell: custom labels with the couple's names for wedding, debut or corporate giveaways (1 ml or 2 ml in bulk).

49Shelf talker for partners

If a salon, café or boutique sells your sets for you, a small standing card on their counter with a QR to your shop.

50Discovery six map

A 2 × 3 map under the vials, so customers know which hole is which perfume.
