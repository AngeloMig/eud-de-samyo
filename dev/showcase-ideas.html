Product section: ideas

Working prototypes in the Perfume showcase. Each is a theme-editor setting, off on your homepage until you pick it. Frames are live pages: click, drag and add to bag inside them. Products and prices are preview demos.

0

Now

The section as it is on the homepage today.

1

Star rating from real reviews

example data

Stars, the score and the review count under the name. It reads Shopify's standard reviews.rating / reviews.rating_count fields that review apps (Judge.me, Shopify Product Reviews…) fill in, and shows nothing when there are no reviews. The numbers in this frame are examples, not real reviews. Setting: Show star rating.

2

Compare notes

A "⇄ Compare notes" button under the note chips opens two perfumes side by side (pick the second from a list); notes they share are highlighted. Built from what is already on the page, works with the keyboard and Esc. Opens by itself in these frames. Setting: Show Compare notes.

3

Mini bag after Add to bag

After the spray, a small panel slides in with the bottle, size, price, bag subtotal and item count, plus View bag / Checkout. Closes after 6 s (stays while hovered), on × or Esc. On phones it sits at the bottom. Adds itself in these frames. Setting: After adding → Slide in a mini bag.

4

"Buy at FragranceX" for perfumes you don't stock

A block with a retailer link instead of a product shows "Buy at FragranceX ↗" (opens in a new tab). The disclosure line and rel="sponsored" appear only when the block is ticked as an approved affiliate tracking link. Shown on Stronger With You Intensely here. Settings: per perfume, Sold elsewhere (affiliate).

5

Sharper bottle photos

content

The chosen bottle is about 410 px tall on a laptop, so on a sharp screen it needs about 900 px of height. Today: Dior Homme Intense 951 px (sharp), Armani 480 px, Sauvage 447 px, D&G 266 px (soft). Send larger cut-outs and the section stays crisp when bottles come forward.

6

Link the real products

content

Pick the Product in each Perfume block once products exist. Sizes, prices, stock (sold-out sizes crossed out) and the in-place Add to bag then come from Shopify; the demo values here disappear.
