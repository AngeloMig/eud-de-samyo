Investor calculator

Two ways to plan investor money. First, one investor paying for one bottle. Below, several investors (1 to 5) funding the whole stock. Each "round" = the stock bought with the money, sold until the bottles are empty. Each investor earns a % of their money per round, up to a total payout, then gets their money back and leaves. Figures use the per-bottle results from the decant price calculator (23 Nov 2026 prices); change any number to test your own case. Not financial advice.

One bottle, one investor

An investor pays for one bottle. When that bottle is empty, they get their money back plus a % (e.g. ₱6,200 + 20% = ₱7,440). Pick a perfume to fill in its prices, or type your own.

"Costs per vial" covers the vial, label and its share of the box and delivery; "fees" are payment, Shopify and a small allowance, charged on the price. Both come from the price calculator (still estimates). The bottle cost itself is counted separately, so profit here is after paying back the bottle.

Several investors

Return tiers Each investor's rate fills in from the amount they put in. You can still type a different rate on any investor; "Use tier" switches it back.

Whole stock: their money buys part of all your stock; they get the return each time a round sells out, for the number of rounds, then their money back (₱100,000 at 20% for 3 rounds → ₱160,000). "Reinvested" grows their money each round instead. One bottle: they pay for one bottle; choosing the perfume fills in its price, vial price and vial count, and when that bottle is empty they get their money back plus the return. Everything under each row is worked out automatically.

Business assumptions

Defaults come from the 9-bottle example (every perfume except Oud Wood, sold out as 1 ml sets): ₱76,806 of bottles → ₱239,400 sales, ₱121,533 profit after bottles and every per-order cost (vials, labels, box, fees, delivery), about 285 orders. Those per-order costs are still estimates in the price calculator.
