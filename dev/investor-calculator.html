Investor calculator

Two ways to plan investor money. First, one investor paying for one bottle. Below, several investors (1 to 5) funding the whole stock or single bottles. Each "round" = the stock bought with the money, sold until that round's share is sold. Each investor earns a fixed % of their money, or a share of the profit, up to a total payout, then gets their money back and leaves. Change any number to test your own case. Estimates, not financial advice.

One bottle, one investor

An investor pays for one bottle. When that bottle is empty, they get their money back plus a % (e.g. ₱6,200 + 20% = ₱7,440). Pick a perfume to fill in its prices, or type your own.

"Costs per vial" covers the vial, label and its share of the box and delivery; "fees" are payment, Shopify and a small allowance, charged on the price. Bottle cost = price × (1 − wholesale discount) × (1 + import). Profit here is after paying for the bottle.

Several investors

Return tiers Each investor's rate fills in from the amount they put in. You can still type a different rate on any investor; "Use tier" switches it back.

Whole stock: their money buys part of all your stock; they get a return each time a round sells, for the number of rounds, then their money back (₱100,000 at 20% for 3 rounds → ₱160,000). "Reinvested" grows their money each round instead. One bottle: they pay for one bottle; when it is empty they get their money back plus the return. Return type: "Fixed %" pays the rate on their money; "% of profit" pays their share of the profit their money made, never more than the cap (from the rate, or a ₱ cap you type).

Business assumptions

Results

Round by round

Whole-stock rounds. Unsold stock is carried to the next round and sold first, so it only delays cash. Your monthly orders are shared with the bottle investors' bottles (see the note under the cash table).

Each investor

Monthly cash check

Shared selling: every month you sell your "orders per month". They are split between the current stock round and each investor bottle in proportion to the stock value each still has to sell, worked out again whenever a round starts or a bottle empties; one bottle never sells faster than "vials of one scent sold per month". Fixed costs are charged per order (fixed costs ÷ orders per month).
