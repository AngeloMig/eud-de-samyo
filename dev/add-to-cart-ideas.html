Add to bag: 10 animation ideas

Click each button. Every one runs the same story: adding (about 0.9 s), then added (2 s), then back. Each respects "reduce motion" (no movement, just the label change). Today's showcase uses idea 1.

1

Spritz (current)

A cone of fine droplets and a mist cloud puff from the atomizer side, pulsing while adding, a bigger burst when added.

Bag2
2

Bottle to bag

A tiny bottle lifts off the button and arcs into the Bag in the header; the Bag bumps and its count goes up. Tells people where it went.

Bag2
3

Juice fills the button

The button fills left to right with the perfume's own colour, with tiny rising bubbles, like a bottle being filled; then "Added".

Bag2
4

Colour bloom

A bloom of the perfume's accent colour spreads from where you tapped, filling the button; calm and premium.

Bag2
5

Scent trail

A handwritten word of its main note ("lavender") writes itself above the button and drifts away like a trail of scent.

Bag2
6

Morph to a ring

The button shrinks into a circle with a thin spinning ring while adding, then opens back out, green, as "Added".

Bag2
7

Note petals

The perfume's notes (lemon, pepper, lavender, vanilla) burst out as little petals and float down. Playful; best for gifting moments.

Bag2
8

Cap twist

A tiny bottle inside the button twists its cap off and on while adding: a small, crafted detail, quiet enough to use everywhere.

Bag2
9

Rolling label

The label rolls up through "Adding…" to "Added ✓", like the header's link hover. The most minimal and NOHO-like.

Bag2
10

Shimmer + spring

The button presses in, a soft shimmer sweeps across while adding, and it springs back when added. On phones it gives a tiny vibration too.
