Ten ways to sell decants

Ten creative takes on the homepage Decants section, each a working mock-up: hover, click and try them. All use the same vial photos and the same bundles (3 × 1 ml, 6 × 1 ml, 3 × 2 ml, 6 × 2 ml, 2 × 5 ml, 1 × 10 ml). Prices are placeholders; set the real ones with the pricing calculator (dev/decant-pricing.html).
