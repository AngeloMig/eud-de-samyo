Footer: 10 perfume ideas

Ten footers that only a perfume shop would have. Each keeps the real footer's content (email signup, Shop and Help links, contact, the eau de sangyó wordmark) and turns it into something from the world of fragrance. Hover and click: most of them move. Links and text are placeholders.

1Scent pyramid

The footer is built like a fragrance's notes: top notes (the email signup, the first thing you notice), heart (Shop and Help links), base (contact and the big wordmark that lingers). Each tier is wider than the one above.

6The bottle label

The whole footer is a classic perfume label: name, "No. 01", the city, 100 ml. The links are listed like the ingredients, and the bottom line reads like a batch number with the year and copyright.
