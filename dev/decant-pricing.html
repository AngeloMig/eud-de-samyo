Decant pricing: contribution before ads and fixed overhead

Target: a 45–50% fully costed contribution margin on every in-house decant set, after the set discount, for every possible mix of scents. Margin = (selling price − all variable order costs) ÷ selling price. Advertising (a monthly budget spread over your orders), the Shopify plan, apps and other fixed costs are kept separate. Full-bottle affiliate earnings are calculated on their own (section 8). Bottle prices: FragranceX in pesos, 22 Nov 2026 (100 ml tester where listed). Nothing here changes the live store.

Every cost that grows with each order. Filling and packing labour are set to ₱0 because you handle them yourself. Tick “Confirmed” once a number comes from your supplier, courier, payment provider or Shopify plan; until then it is an estimate.

3b · Profit per bottle

What one full bottle earns if you pour all of it into one size and sell it in that size’s set (1 and 2 ml as trios, 5 ml as duos, 10 ml on its own), at the recommended prices. Usable ml = bottle ml × (1 − transfer loss); leftover ml that doesn’t fill a whole vial is not counted. Contribution before ads and fixed overhead, then after your ad spend per order. “Return” = profit ÷ what the bottle cost you (incl. shipping & import).

Monthly costs that don’t grow with each order. Spread over your expected orders per month to see operating profit per order. Enter 0 if you have no app or other fixed costs; blank means not set yet.

8 · Full-bottle affiliate earnings (separate)

Full bottles are sold by FragranceX through your CJ link; Samyó never holds the stock. Earnings = FragranceX price × your commission rate. Enter the rate from your CJ account; until then nothing is assumed.

How the calculator works. Item total = vial prices minus the bundle discount, then any additional discount in “Try a set.” Payment total = discounted items + delivery paid by the customer.

Perfume cost per usable ml = bottle cost including inbound shipping and import ÷ (bottle ml × usable percentage after transfer loss). Order costs include the perfume used, vials, labels, packing materials and any labour entered. Filling and packing labour currently default to ₱0.

Payment processing fee = payment total × processing rate + the payment fee per order. Shopify transaction fee = payment total × Shopify rate. Refund & replacement reserve = discounted item total × reserve rate. The reserve is money set aside for problems.

Delivery we cover = the courier fee when the discounted item total reaches the free-delivery threshold, plus any extra delivery support entered. Item protection is separate. Customer-paid delivery covers the courier fee and is excluded from our delivery expense; payment fees on that money are included in the payment rows.

Contribution = discounted item total − all order costs. Margin = contribution ÷ discounted item total. Ads and fixed overhead are deducted separately. Recommended vial prices are rounded up to ₱5 and checked against the target margin after the bundle discount; the additional discount only affects your selected-set example. VAT and income tax are not included.
