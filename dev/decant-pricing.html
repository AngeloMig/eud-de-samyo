Tax

How to read this: tax is worked out once a year from this plan (monthly sales × 12, or the yearly sales you type) and kept separate from every table above it. The defaults (not VAT-registered, no income tax) change nothing.

  • VAT registration is required once yearly gross sales exceed ₱3,000,000. If you are VAT-registered, your store prices include 12% output VAT: 12/112 of every sale is owed to the BIR and is not profit. The VAT you paid when importing bottles becomes claimable input VAT; its share of the import cost comes from the duty and VAT inputs in “Work it out” (VAT share of the order value = (1 + duty) × VAT). Other input VAT (vials, fees, Shopify) is not counted.
  • 8% option: for individuals who are not VAT-registered with gross sales up to ₱3,000,000. 8% of gross sales above ₱250,000, in place of both the 3% percentage tax and graduated income tax.
  • 3% percentage tax + graduated income tax: 3% of gross sales (not charged if VAT-registered), plus income tax on taxable income: up to ₱250,000 nil; to ₱400,000 15% of the excess; to ₱800,000 ₱22,500 + 20%; to ₱2,000,000 ₱102,500 + 25%; to ₱8,000,000 ₱402,500 + 30%; above, ₱2,202,500 + 35%. Taxable income with the 40% OSD = 60% of gross sales; itemized = this plan’s operating profit (after the percentage tax).
  • Sales here exclude delivery the customer pays. How that delivery charge is taxed should be confirmed with a CPA; the calculator does not change it automatically. Nothing here is tax advice.

Profit per bottle

How to read this: each cell is one full bottle poured into one size and sold at the recommended prices. Bold = contribution before ads; then profit after ads, operating profit after ads and fixed overhead, and that operating profit as a return on what the bottle cost you. Smaller lines show how it adds up; the last line compares your existing price (draft recommendation for the two additions).

Sets mix different scents, so each vial carries its share of one order’s costs: a third of a trio for 1 and 2 ml, half a duo for 5 ml, a whole order for 10 ml. Order share = vials ÷ vials per set (the number of orders’ worth of box, fees, ads and overhead the bottle carries). Usable ml = bottle ml × (1 − transfer loss); the transfer loss is already inside the perfume cost per usable ml. Usable ml that can’t fill one more vial is charged once as leftover perfume (waste).

Contribution = sales − all variable costs: perfume in the vials, leftover perfume (waste), vials, labels, filling, the order-cost share (box, packing, payment fee per order, delivery, protection) and the payment, Shopify and reserve %. Profit after ads = contribution − ads per order × order share. Operating profit = profit after ads − fixed overhead per order × order share. Red when below ₱0. Return = the figure ÷ landed wholesale bottle cost (including import). Total cash spent = sales − operating profit (every cost paid for this bottle’s sales, incl. ads and overhead); return on it = operating profit ÷ total cash spent.

How the calculator works. Item total = vial prices minus the bundle discount, then any additional discount in “Try a set.” Payment total = discounted items + delivery paid by the customer.

Bottle cost = the wholesale list’s Cost (cheaper tester when chosen) × (1 − extra supplier discount) × (1 + import %). The wholesale reduction is already included in Cost; it is not deducted again. Perfume cost per usable ml = bottle cost ÷ (bottle ml × usable percentage after transfer loss). Order costs include the perfume used, vials, labels, packing materials and any labour entered. Filling and packing labour currently default to ₱0.

Payment processing rate = e-wallet rate + card share × (card rate − e-wallet rate). Payment processing fee = payment total × that rate + the payment fee per order. Shopify transaction fee = payment total × Shopify rate. Refund & replacement reserve = discounted item total × reserve rate. The reserve is money set aside for problems.

Delivery we cover = the courier fee when the discounted item total reaches the free-delivery threshold, plus any extra delivery support entered. Item protection is separate. Customer-paid delivery covers the courier fee and is excluded from our delivery expense; payment fees on that money are included in the payment rows.

Contribution = discounted item total − all order costs. Margin = contribution ÷ discounted item total. Ads and fixed overhead are deducted separately. Recommended vial prices are rounded up to ₱5 and checked against the target margin after the bundle discount; the additional discount only affects your selected-set example. Tax is kept separate in the monthly plan: by default nothing is deducted (not VAT-registered, no income tax). Profit per bottle adds an “after tax (estimate)” line only when a tax option is on, using the plan’s effective tax rate on operating profit.

Profit per bottle: contribution = sales − all variable costs, including leftover perfume (usable ml that can’t fill one more vial × perfume cost per usable ml). Ads and fixed overhead are allocated by order share (vials ÷ vials per set). Operating figures are tagged “incomplete” while a fixed cost is blank and “estimate” while one is not confirmed.
