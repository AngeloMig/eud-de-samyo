Store name ideas

Ten names for a premium designer-perfume shop in the Philippines, most rooted in Filipino words for scent, breeze and flowers. Each is shown as a plain wordmark so you can feel it as a logo. Nothing here is checked for trademarks or business registrations yet; check your favourite before building on it, and I can check which domains are available.

01ylang
My pick

"EE-lang"

From ylang-ylang, the flower that comes from the Philippines and sits in many classic perfumes. Short, global, unmistakably perfume, and quietly Filipino.

02simoy
My pick

"see-MOY" · Tagalog: a gentle breeze

The air that carries a scent as someone passes, which is exactly what perfume is about. Soft and elegant to say.

03dama
My pick

"DAH-ma"

From dama de noche, the night-blooming jasmine of Filipino gardens; also "lady" in Spanish. Two syllables, timeless, easy on a bottle or a bag.

04halimuyak

"ha-li-MOO-yak" · Tagalog: fragrance, sweet scent

The poetic Filipino word for fragrance. Long and stately; strong for a luxury feel, harder to type.

05bangó

"ba-NGO" · Tagalog: a good smell

Warm, short and friendly; every Filipino gets it instantly. Playful rather than formal.

06haplos

"HAP-los" · Tagalog: a gentle touch, a caress

Intimate and sensual, like a fragrance on skin. Distinctive, unusual as a shop name.

07hiraya

"hee-RAH-ya" · Tagalog: the fruit of one's hopes

Dreamy and beautiful. Note: widely used by Filipino brands, so harder to own.

08maison amihan

Amihan: the cool northeast wind

"Maison" gives a French house feel; Amihan brings the cool season breeze. Grand, but long.

09esencia

"e-SEN-sya" · essence

The Spanish-Filipino word for essence, as in perfume extract. Classic and clear; less unique.

10sillage & co.

"see-YAZH" · the trail a perfume leaves

The insider word for a scent's trail (and a section on your fragrance pages). Very "perfume people"; a common word, so hard to trademark alone.
