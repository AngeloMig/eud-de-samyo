Sections Samyo still needs

The homepage has the hero, the Samyó edit, Shop by scent, the brands strip and the footer. To sell well (and to be trusted as a new perfume shop), it still needs a few homepage sections and, more importantly, the pages behind them: a product page, collections with filters and the quiz the hero's "Find your scent" button points to. Mock-ups use your bottle photos; prices and wording are examples.
