Decants section: 10 ideas

Ten new directions for the homepage Decants section, all different from the first round (dev/decant-home-ideas.html). Each mock uses the real sets and prices, the vial cut-outs drawn to scale (1 ml shortest, 10 ml tallest), and the rule that 1, 2 and 5 ml vials are sold only in sets while 10 ml can be bought alone.
